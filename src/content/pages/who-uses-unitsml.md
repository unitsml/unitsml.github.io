shipbuilding, industrial automation, scientific publishing, and markup language design.

## Why organizations choose UnitsML

- **Unambiguous** — every unit is defined by identity, dimension, and system, not just a symbol.
- **SI-aligned** — built on the seven SI base quantities and the BIPM SI Brochure.
- **Machine-readable** — XML schemas validate unit usage in any XML document.
- **Interoperable** — cross-references to BIPM SI Digital Framework, UCUM, QUDT, and IEC CDD.
- **Established** — UnitsML since 2003. 23 strong years and counting, under NIST, OASIS, and CalConnect.

## Become an adopter

If your standard or product encodes scientific units, UnitsML can provide the semantic foundation.
