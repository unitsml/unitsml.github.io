# History of UnitsML

UnitsML since 2003 — 23 strong years and counting. A continuous journey of
standards development from a research project at Lawrence Berkeley National
Laboratory to the authoritative XML units standard maintained by CalConnect
TC UNITS.

<HistoryTimeline />

## Key milestones

- **1998** — Frank Olken and John McCarthy begin encoding units in XML at LBNL.
- **2003** — NIST's "Units Markup Language" presentation at the Sixth Open Forum on Metadata Registries.
- **2006** — OASIS Technical Committee formed; first teleconference July 12.
- **2006–2010** — NIST publishes 26 schema revisions (0.7 through 0.9.19), adding conversions, root-unit enumerations, dimensions, and counted items.
- **2011** — UnitsML v1.0-csd04 approved.
- **2022** — CalConnect TC UNITS established; SciDataCon and CIPM presentations.
- **2025** — UnitsDB 1.0 and 2.0 released with BIPM Digital SI Framework cross-references.
