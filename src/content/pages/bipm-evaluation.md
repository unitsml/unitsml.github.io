# BIPM Digital SI Evaluation

<div class="eval-hero">
  <span class="eval-hero-badge">
    <svg width="14" height="14" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2"><path d="M12 22s8-4 8-10V5l-8-3-8 3v7c0 6 8 10 8 10z"/></svg>
    CIPM Consultation &middot; 12 September 2022
  </span>
  <p class="eval-hero-lead">
    During the development of the <strong>BIPM Digital SI</strong> program, the
    International Committee for Weights and Measures (CIPM) formally consulted
    owners of unit representation systems against a set of technical, management,
    and interoperability criteria. <strong>UnitsML was one of the consulted
    projects</strong> — and its responses, submitted through CalConnect TC UNITS,
    demonstrate a mature, SI-aligned, and sustainably governed representation system.
  </p>
</div>

## Unit coverage

The CIPM's first technical criterion examined how completely a representation system
covers the units that matter to the metrology community.

<div class="eval-rows">
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>SI Brochure (9th edition) units</h4>
      <p>SI units and non-SI units defined in the 9th edition of the BIPM SI Brochure, current and previous versions.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-yes"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="3.5"><polyline points="20 6 9 17 4 12"/></svg>Yes</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>Mises en Pratique (MeP) units</h4>
      <p>Units provided in the Mises en Pratique accompanying the SI.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-yes"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="3.5"><polyline points="20 6 9 17 4 12"/></svg>Yes</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>KCDB 2.0 CMC units</h4>
      <p>Units used in Calibration and Measurement Capability (CMC) data in the KCDB 2.0 database. UnitsML supports over 400 units, covering the units used in KCDB 2.0.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-badge-alt">400+ units</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>SI construct rules</h4>
      <p>Conformance to the SI Brochure rules for standard constructs of SI units, with non-standard constructs flagged. UnitsDB already represents SI-acceptable units as such.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-badge-alt">Conformant</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>Unicode / UTF-8 encoding</h4>
      <p>UnitsDB provides Unicode encodings for the representation of units.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-yes"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="3.5"><polyline points="20 6 9 17 4 12"/></svg>Yes</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>English-language data and documentation</h4>
      <p>Human-readable content and all documentation are provided in English.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-yes"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="3.5"><polyline points="20 6 9 17 4 12"/></svg>Yes</span>

## Governance and long-term maintenance

The CIPM's management criteria addressed documentation, cooperation with the
metrology community, and the availability and maintenance of the system.

<div class="eval-cards">
  <div class="eval-card">
    <div class="eval-card-icon">
      <svg width="22" height="22" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8"><rect x="3" y="4" width="18" height="18" rx="2"/><line x1="16" y1="2" x2="16" y2="6"/><line x1="8" y1="2" x2="8" y2="6"/><line x1="3" y1="10" x2="21" y2="10"/></svg>
    </div>
    <h4>10-year maintenance commitment</h4>
    <p>CalConnect TC UNITS is the advisory body that manages UnitsML and UnitsDB, with user support, maintenance, and management of the system guaranteed as far as possible for at least ten years. All CalConnect technical committees are governed under the CalConnect Directives, and CalConnect holds liaisons to many international SDOs, including ISO and ITU.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-card">
    <div class="eval-card-icon">
      <svg width="22" height="22" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8"><path d="M14 2H6a2 2 0 00-2 2v16a2 2 0 002 2h12a2 2 0 002-2V8z"/><polyline points="14 2 14 8 20 8"/><line x1="16" y1="13" x2="8" y2="13"/><line x1="16" y1="17" x2="8" y2="17"/></svg>
    </div>
    <h4>ISO 19135 register methodology</h4>
    <p>TC UNITS adopts the ISO 19135 methodology for management of the UnitsDB register — an internationally recognized standard for register management — providing orderly processes for additions, corrections, and release schedules within a quality system.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-card">
    <div class="eval-card-icon">
      <svg width="22" height="22" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8"><circle cx="12" cy="12" r="10"/><line x1="2" y1="12" x2="22" y2="12"/><path d="M12 2a15.3 15.3 0 014 10 15.3 15.3 0 01-4 10 15.3 15.3 0 01-4-10 15.3 15.3 0 014-10z"/></svg>
    </div>
    <h4>ISO standardization plan</h4>
    <p>TC UNITS plans to submit UnitsML to ISO for international standardization — ensuring that full responsibility for the representation system can be assumed by a new owner should the current one ever stop its maintenance and support.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-card">
    <div class="eval-card-icon">
      <svg width="22" height="22" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8"><polyline points="1 4 1 10 7 10"/><path d="M3.51 15a9 9 0 102.13-9.36L1 10"/></svg>
    </div>
    <h4>Backward-compatibility commitment</h4>
    <p>UnitsML committed to work with the CIPM so that future updates of the representation system remain backward compatible or provide the means for easy migration from old to newer versions, with any breaking changes made clear in documentation and metadata.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-card">
    <div class="eval-card-icon">
      <svg width="22" height="22" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8"><polyline points="16 18 22 12 16 6"/><polyline points="8 6 2 12 8 18"/></svg>
    </div>
    <h4>Version-controlled specifications</h4>
    <p>All specifications are under version control with a tracked history of changes, and are openly provided on GitHub. All properties germane to metrological data are well defined.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-card">
    <div class="eval-card-icon">
      <svg width="22" height="22" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8"><rect x="3" y="11" width="18" height="11" rx="2"/><path d="M7 11V7a5 5 0 019.9-1"/></svg>
    </div>
    <h4>Open license</h4>
    <p>UnitsML and its associated technologies are published openly under the CalConnect/OASIS license, granting the CIPM and all users maximum flexibility for reuse, incorporation in applications, and availability of local copies.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Open data and interoperability

Beyond the mandatory criteria, the consultation recorded further technical
strengths of the representation system.

<div class="eval-rows">
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>Alignment with FAIR principles</h4>
      <p>UnitsML data is Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-yes"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="3.5"><polyline points="20 6 9 17 4 12"/></svg>Yes</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>Alignment with 5-Star Data (Semantic Web)</h4>
      <p>UnitsDB is published as linked open data at the highest level of the 5-Star scheme.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-yes"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="3.5"><polyline points="20 6 9 17 4 12"/></svg>Yes</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>Persistent identifiers</h4>
      <p>UnitsDB provides persistent IDs for every unit and kind of quantity.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-badge-alt">Provided</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>International System of Quantities (ISQ)</h4>
      <p>UnitsML already supports most ISO 80000 quantities, linking kinds of quantities to applicable units.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-badge-alt">Supported</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>Conversion between units</h4>
      <p>UnitsML supports execution and evaluation of conversions between units associated with the same kind of quantity.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-badge-alt">Supported</span>
  </div>
  <div class="eval-row">
    <div class="eval-row-body">
      <h4>Open community register</h4>
      <p>The UnitsDB register is an open register on GitHub that accepts requests from community developers. Missing units needed by the metrology community can be added through CalConnect TC UNITS.</p>
    </div>
    <span class="eval-badge-alt">Open</span>
  </div>
</div>

## Standing commitments to the metrology community

<div class="eval-commit">
  <div class="eval-commit-grid">
    <div class="eval-commit-item">
      <h4>Adoption of SI definitions</h4>
      <p>Unit definitions can be adapted to match the latest definitions in the 9th edition of the BIPM SI Brochure as it evolves.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="eval-commit-item">
      <h4>Extensibility for metrology</h4>
      <p>Any missing units needed by the metrology community can be added through CalConnect TC UNITS.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="eval-commit-item">
      <h4>Collaboration with the CIPM</h4>
      <p>TC UNITS is happy to collaborate with the CIPM and other users on technical developments.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="eval-commit-item">
      <h4>Continuity through ISO</h4>
      <p>International standardization of UnitsML through ISO safeguards the system's long-term ownership and continuity.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
